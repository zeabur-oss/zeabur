Each entry is a pattern and its fix. One hit can be chance; a cluster is the tell. Vale catches the word-level ones; the structural ones need reading. Narrative articles have more in the `story-writing` skill's `ai-tells.md`.

## Both languages

- **Manufactured contrast.** 「不是 X，而是 Y」「不只是 X，更是 Y」, "not just X, but Y", "It's not X, it's Y". Fix: say Y.
- **Defensive disclaimers.** 「不代表」「不宣稱」「並不意味」, "this does not imply". Fix: delete, or state the fact. 「不一定能」 for an uncertain outcome and 「不保證」 for a commitment are not tells.
- **Lists of three by reflex.** Three items in every sentence. Fix: keep the ones that are true; two or four are fine.
- **Announcing and summarising.** 「以下說明…」「值得注意的是」「總的來說」, "This page shows…", "In summary". Fix: start with the fact; end on the last fact.
- **Inflated words.** 至關重要、不可或缺、賦能、打造、無縫、一站式、深入探討; *delve, pivotal, crucial, seamless, robust, leverage, showcase, underscore, tapestry, intricate, meticulous*. Fix: the plain word or the concrete fact.
- **Copula avoidance.** 「作為」「扮演…的角色」, *serves as*, *stands as*. Fix: 是 / *is*.
- **Smoothed-out detail.** A number or name in the source becomes 「很多」「各種」, *various*, *a range of*. Fix: put the number back.
- **Bold everywhere, emoji bullets, "**Label** — explanation" lists.** Fix: prose, or a plain list.

## Chinese

- **English sentence skeleton**: 它 in every clause, 被 by default, *until / if / to ensure* clauses after the main clause, 當…的時候, 最…之一. See `zh.md`, "Sentence structure".
- **Stacked short sentences**: a 。 after every clause, so the paragraph reads as translated English. Fix: join clauses that share a subject with ，.
- **Clipped endings next to stiff words**: 「不會跟著換」 beside 「予以更新」. Fix: one register, plain written Chinese with two-syllable verbs.

## English

- **Participle tails**: ", ensuring…", ", highlighting…", ", making it easy to…". Instruction-tuned models use present participial clauses at 2 to 5 times the human rate. Fix: a second sentence, or cut.
- **Nominalisations**: "perform an analysis of" → "analyze". Models use them at 1.5 to 2 times the human rate.
- **Em-dash asides**, two or more in a sentence. Fix: commas, parentheses or a second sentence.
- **Sentence-opening connectives**: *Additionally, Furthermore, Moreover, Notably*. Fix: drop them; order the sentences so the link is plain.
- **Title Case Headings.** Fix: sentence case.
