# Landing and product pages

Copy on a landing page, a product page or a release page has one job: say what the product does
for the reader, in terms they can check. The rest of this skill still applies.

- **Concrete over claimed.** Name the task the reader finishes and what they see when it is done
  ("Deploy a Git repository and get an HTTPS address"). An adjective that the page cannot prove
  (fast, powerful, effortless) goes, or becomes the number or behaviour behind it.
- **No marketing words.** No *revolutionary*, *next-generation*, *all-in-one*, 「打造」「賦能」「一站式」.
  The words in [`ai-tells.md`](ai-tells.md) are the first to cut.
- **No manufactured contrast.** No "not X but Y" or 「不是 X，而是 Y」 to make a point; state Y.
- **No hedging paragraphs.** A limit that changes what the reader does sits next to the feature it
  limits. A general disclaimer goes.
- **Other products.** Describe ours by what it does. Never belittle another platform; a
  comparison names the concrete difference and where it comes from.
- **Goals are goals.** Write a plan as a plan. A feature that is not released does not appear as
  available.
- **Same claims in every language.** Each locale reads as its own text (step 3 of `SKILL.md`), and
  all of them make the same claims with the same numbers and links.
- **Images show the product.** A screenshot is of the real product at the state the text
  describes. The repository's own skill says where screenshots come from and how they are sized.
