Zeabur is starting in {{ .Release.Namespace }}:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status deployment/{{ include "zeabur.fullname" . }}

Then open {{ .Values.publicUrl }} and sign in through {{ .Values.issuer.url }}.
{{- if not (or .Values.ingress.enabled .Values.httpRoute.enabled) }}

Neither ingress.enabled nor httpRoute.enabled is set: route {{ .Values.publicUrl }} to the
Service {{ include "zeabur.fullname" . }} (port {{ .Values.service.port }}) yourself.
{{- end }}
{{- if .Values.memberRoles.enabled }}

Zeabur gives members their rights with RoleBindings and ClusterRoleBindings it makes itself.
`helm uninstall` leaves them. After it, remove them with:

  kubectl delete rolebindings,clusterrolebindings --all-namespaces \
    -l app.kubernetes.io/managed-by=zeabur,zeabur.com/member
{{- end }}
